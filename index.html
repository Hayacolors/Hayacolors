The Art of Handcrafted Heritage

At Aura Ethnic, we bridge traditional Indian artisan clusters with contemporary fashion aesthetics, preserving centuries-old Lucknowi Shadow embroidery and pure weave craftsmanship.

Authentic Lucknowi Chikankari

Each Chikankari garment undergoes a meticulous 32-stitch process executed by master craftswomen in Lucknow. From Bakhiya (shadow stitch) to Phanda (millet-shaped knots), every piece is a unique masterpiece of patience and beauty.

Pure Cotton & Suti Collection

We source 100% long-staple breathable cotton, block-printed with non-toxic organic dyes to guarantee comfort, longevity, and timeless style for everyday wear.
